In 2020, a small group of university students started Mulai Disini Project — a platform to bring non-academic education to SMA/SMK students across Indonesia, through sharing, mentorship, and self-development. Berangkat dari ajakan berbagi, memaknai dengan kesungguhan hati, tanami akal budi, dan kita mulai di sini.

Why it started

School teaches the syllabus. Life asks for a lot more.

Technology made learning possible anywhere, anytime, for anyone. But learning was never only academic — and many students stepped into university without ever building the habits, self-awareness, or coping tools that transition demanded of them.

Mulai Disini Project paired rookies (SMA/SMK students) with buddy mentors for a three-month curriculum in self-development — reading, time management, motivation, and more — plus talks and public sharing sessions on Instagram Live.

Why We Exist

Where you're born still decides what education you get.

Across Indonesia and much of the developing world, quality education still depends heavily on geography — on whether a community has the buildings, the funding, or the programs it needs. Formal schooling rarely closes that gap on its own.

White Bridges closes it two ways. Our clubs bring people together around books, film, and art — low-barrier, joyful, community-first. Our foundation then turns that community's creative output into lasting impact: funded scholarships, new schools, museum collaborations, and support that reaches students far beyond our own city.

It's one mission carried by two engines — a club that builds community, and a foundation that builds outcomes.
